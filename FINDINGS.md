# Business Insights & Recommendations: Customer Churn

## 🔍 Top 3 Findings
1. **The Biggest Churn Driver:** We found that customers on **month-to-month** contracts have a churn rate of **26.51%**, making them the highest risk group.
2. **Revenue Impact:** The business is currently losing **$8,463,309.54** in Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) specifically from churned customers.
3. **The "Sticky" Factor:** Customers who use **Tech Support / Online Security** have a significantly lower churn rate compared to those who don't. Furthermore, churn drops drastically as customers move past their first 12 months of tenure.

## 💡 Recommendations for the Business
Based on the data, I recommend the following actions:
1. **Incentivize 1-Year Contracts:** Since month-to-month users churn the fastest (26.51% vs 12.65% for 1-year), marketing should offer a 10% discount to upgrade to an annual plan.
2. **Bundle Services:** Give away the first 3 months of Tech Support for free to new users, as our data shows this service drastically improves long-term retention.
3. **Proactive Outreach:** Support teams should automatically contact month-to-month customers early in their lifecycle to transition them to longer, stickier contracts.
